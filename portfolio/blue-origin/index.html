Solution

I defined and championed the vision for a unified design system and knowledge platform, aligning design goals with Blue Origin's mission and operational priorities. The Blue Design System was architected in Figma and integrated with React, enabling seamless collaboration and rapid prototyping. Custom Figma plugins automated standards enforcement and accelerated development workflows, while a scalable information architecture supported future growth.

BlueGPT: AI-Powered Agent Platform

I led the design and deployment of BlueGPT, a secure LLM gateway, agent marketplace, and multi-agent orchestration platform that transformed how Blue Origin engineers work. Built on AWS infrastructure, BlueGPT empowers every employee to create and deploy specialized AI agents, providing access to proprietary knowledge bases, custom tools, and leading foundation models.

Platform Architecture

BlueGPT was architected as a comprehensive AI ecosystem using:

  • Amazon Bedrock for foundation model access, knowledge bases, and AgentCore capabilities with hierarchical memory systems
  • Strands Agents SDK for model-driven AI agent orchestration, enabling LLM reasoning over rigid workflows
  • Amazon EKS for containerized agent runtime environments and microservice capabilities
  • Amazon OpenSearch for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) knowledge bases
  • Amazon RDS for general purpose databases
  • AWS Lambda for serverless workflow automations

This architecture follows a hierarchical coordination model with specialized domain agents that adapt their behavior based on query complexity and context, reducing AI application development time from weeks to hours (10x improvement).

TEAREx: World's First AI Agent-Designed Lunar Hardware

The most groundbreaking application of BlueGPT was the development of TEAREx (Thermal Energy Advanced Regolith Extraction), what Blue Origin believes to be the world's first AI agent-designed hardware built to operate on the lunar surface. The In-Space Systems team assembled a specialized team of AI agents — supervisors, librarians, requirements agents, design agents, and analysis agents — to work alongside just 2-3 human engineers.

The breakthrough was automated iterative design loops. Connected to AI-native tools, the agents ran complex physics simulations on GPU-accelerated Amazon EC2 instances, evaluated results against requirements, iterated on the design, and executed until specifications were met — autonomously. This thermal battery system was designed to help lunar systems survive the brutal two-week lunar night by extracting heat energy to and from lunar soil.

Results:

  • 90% reduction in hardware development time — from years to days, concept to 3D-printed part
  • 6x acceleration of analysis workflows — tasks that took 4 days now take 4 hours
  • Mass-optimized designs through automated topology optimization cycles
  • Scaled specialized expertise — small teams of 2-3 working with AI agents to deliver the work of dozens

Enterprise Impact

Beyond TEAREx, BlueGPT agents are being applied across the organization:

  • Manufacturing teams resolve non-conformances 70% faster and improve work orders
  • Operations teams communicate design changes with suppliers through AI intermediaries
  • Engineers use agents to write code and accelerate approvals
  • Agents live in BlueGPT's marketplace, ready to be reused, recombined, and applied to tubes, barrels, harnesses, assemblies, and eventually entire vehicles

The platform institutionalized decades of informal expertise, making specialized aerospace knowledge accessible to teams across the organization. With strict security, trade, and export control requirements, BlueGPT ensures proprietary knowledge remains protected while democratizing innovation at scale.
